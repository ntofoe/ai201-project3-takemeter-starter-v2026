# Label definitions for TakeMeter (r/nba top-level comments).
# Copied from README > Label Taxonomy. Labels are defined by what a post does, not its tone.

analysis: makes an arguable claim about basketball and supports it with a specific on-court fact (a stat, score, minutes, a quote, or a specific described play) offered as the reason; tone and insults are ignored, and contract, salary, age, or draft position alone do not count as support
hot_take: makes an arguable claim about basketball, including a prediction, but offers no specific on-court fact as support; vague descriptions like "so many boneheaded moves" are not support
reaction: makes no arguable claim about basketball; a feeling, joke, meme, taunt, or a restatement of what happened, even if it contains a number

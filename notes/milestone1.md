## Confusing comments (copied word for word)
- **6.** "De'Aaron Fox is getting paid close to a quarter of a BILLION dollars to be a basketball terrorist"
  — joke/insult, or claim + specific support? The contract figure is real and checkable, but it isn't being used as evidence for anything.
- **41.** "Chet fucking stinks, Ozempic Abraham Lincoln is a no show once again / 4 points on 1/2 FG in G7 of the WCF? With B2B Finals on the line?"
  — insult, or claim + specific support? The stat line genuinely supports "no show." Tone says one thing, content says another.
- **27.** "Analytically, the 2026 Knicks are the worst team in NBA history."
  — sarcasm, or claim without support? Taken literally it's an unsupported claim; it only works as a joke if you know the "analytics" in-joke.
- **4.** "Spurs woulda won just taking 24 second violations all half / Instead they just kept chucking 3s lmao"
  — sarcastic exaggeration, or a real strategy critique? The first sentence is hyperbole; the second describes something that actually happened in the game.

**Pattern across all four:** a real fact or real critique wrapped in a joke, insult, or sarcasm.
That's the hardest boundary in this community — between "claim + specific support" and
"joke/insult" — and it needs a decision rule in Milestone 2.
